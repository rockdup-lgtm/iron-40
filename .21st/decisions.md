# UI decisions

## Today screen — 2026-10-15
Explored three directions with 21st-ui-explore: **A · Start Now**, B · Week Plan, C · Body First.

**Chosen: A · Start Now.** The next workout is the hero card (title, meta, exercise avatars, Start).
Switching focus is a 2-column tile grid (gym A/B rotation + calisthenics areas) with mini muscle maps,
replacing the chip strip. Stats shrank to a streak pill and a 7-dot week bar; the full exercise list
sits in a collapsible card under the hero.

Why: fastest path from opening the app to training, and the least new logic.
Not chosen: B (needs per-day planning storage), C (small tap targets, gym rotation becomes secondary).
Ideas worth revisiting: B's weekly progress ring; C's recovery-aware suggestion.
